PRIVATE HIRE

A unique venue for your precious moments

Whether it is a birthday, a company meal, a dinner with friends or a special occasion, CỐM Restaurant opens its doors to you for partial or full private hire. Our team works with you to create a tailor-made menu that will take your guests on a journey through the authentic flavors of Vietnam. The colorful, welcoming atmosphere of our restaurant, its wall murals and floral decoration make for a naturally festive setting, with nothing artificial.

Birthdays & Parties

Celebrate your important moments around a generous table. Our chef prepares a festive menu tailored to your wishes, from crispy nems to comforting phở, paired with our selection of mojitos in 11 flavors.

Company meals

Bring your teams together in a setting that takes you elsewhere, a stone's throw from the Palais Royal. Ideal for team lunches, seminars or after-work drinks. We offer packages to suit your budget and number of guests.

Private dinners

For a romantic dinner, a family gathering or a moment with loved ones, enjoy our restaurant in complete privacy. Our attentive, smiling staff will make sure your evening is unforgettable.

PRACTICAL INFORMATION

Organizing your event

Our restaurant can host up to 40 guests when fully privatized. Private hire is available every day, for lunch as well as dinner. We offer menus from €25 per person, with the option of adding our mojito and drinks list. For any request, contact us by phone on 07 61 30 47 10 or through our contact page. We will get back to you as soon as possible to build, together, the event that suits you.
