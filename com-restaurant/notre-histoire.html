OUR STORY

OUR PASSIONATE CHEF

From cinema to the kitchen

A native of Hanoi, a film graduate with a passion for culture and a former manager in the fashion world, drawn to the good things in life, she takes pleasure in sharing her know-how. The layout of her menu is no accident — everything ties back to her passion. It stirs the memories of audiences aged 7 to 77 through film titles and imaginary names that echo the qualities of the characters...

She showcases her produce while respecting the natural flavor of the food. At CỐM, tradition and modesty meet in a delicious dish that brings together a palette of colors and joy.

THE 5 SENSES ON THE PLATE

Hanoi culture

Autumn in Hanoi stirs a restless anticipation among its people, as the rains bring fresh air after the hot days of summer. Hanoi grows gentler and holds a special place in everyone's soul, with unforgettable flavors and scents such as young sticky rice, alstonia and magnolia... These are the natural gifts that earth and sky have given the people of Hanoi. The nostalgia of Hanoi inspires every dish: traditional Northern cuisine, given extra flavor by the modest personal touches of the restaurant's chef.

THE TREASURE OF NORTHERN VIETNAM

What does “CỐM” mean?

Cốm is young sticky rice, also known as green rice. It is harvested in limited quantities, for only 2 months of the year, in autumn. Making cốm is not easy. A special glutinous paddy has to be grown and harvested just as the rice begins to ripen, when the grains hold the most milk.

The grains are carefully sifted, washed and dried over a gentle fire, then pounded in stone mortars. Cốm is a precious rice — it also carries the color that stands for youth. A new generation preserved by its roots, a message to pass on.
