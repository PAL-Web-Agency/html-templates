THE RESTAURANT

The atmosphere at CỐM

In the heart of Paris, our warm and colorful Vietnamese restaurant is an invitation to travel. From the moment you walk in, the fragrant scent of lemongrass, ginger and coriander awakens the senses and gives a foretaste of the delights to come. The atmosphere is intimate and comforting, and our guests leave love notes on our walls for our cooking and for the moments they have spent with us.

Authentic cuisine

Every dish, from steaming phở to spring rolls, celebrates the flavors of Vietnam. Our kitchen serves specialties from Hanoi and brings together the most precious things northern Vietnam has to share with you.

Warm atmosphere

Shimmering colors, floral decoration and a wall mural create an intimate, festive atmosphere. Our attentive, smiling staff add a touch of genuine hospitality.

Mojito Bar

In Vietnam, the Mojito is an institution. Discover our 11 unique flavors, blending classic freshness with bold creativity. Whether you are a purist or looking for something original, explore our selection!

THE NEIGHBORHOOD

From Hanoi to Paris

Right in the heart of Paris, CỐM sits on the corner of Rue des Petits Champs and Rue Vivienne, next to historic landmarks such as the Palais Royal garden and Place de Victoire, and across from the BnF Richelieu museum and Galerie Vivienne. Our restaurant promises a comforting stop in a neighborhood loved for its strolls.
